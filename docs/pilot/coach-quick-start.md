# Sportify for coaches: quick start

Sportify keeps your students' match results, gives each student a skill rating,
and shows a weekly streak and a monthly season table for your batch. This page
covers your first week. It works the same in the phone app and on the website.

## 1. Set up your academy and a batch (5 minutes)

1. Sign up with your name, email and a password.
2. Open **Academy** and tap **Create Academy**. Type your academy's name, for
   example "Velachery Shuttlers".
3. Open the academy and tap **New Batch**. Give it a name your students will
   recognise, for example "Saturday 7am Juniors". You are the batch's coach.

Make one batch for each group that plays together. Ratings are for each
student, so a student can be in more than one batch.

## 2. Invite your students

1. On the batch, tap **Share invite**. On a phone this opens WhatsApp and the
   other share options. On a computer the link is copied, so paste it into
   your class WhatsApp group.
2. A student taps the link, creates an account (or signs in) and taps
   **Join**. They are in the batch straight away.
3. The batch also has a short **invite code** if a student prefers to type it
   in **Academy → Join**.

You can see who has joined under **Members**.

## 3. Record a class's results

After class, open the batch and tap **Record results** (also on the batch's
**Matches** page as **Record class results**).

1. For each match pick **Singles** or **Doubles**, choose the players, and
   enter the score of each game, for example 21-15 and 21-18.
2. Tap **Add match** for the next one. A new match starts with the same
   format as the one before.
3. Tap **Save all**. You can save up to 60 matches at a time.

If one match has a mistake (a missing player, or the same player twice),
nothing is saved and that match is highlighted, so you can fix it and save
again.

**Matches you played in.** Results you enter for your students count straight
away. If you played in a match yourself, it waits until a player on the other
side taps **Confirm** (or another coach of the batch, or the academy owner,
does). Nobody can confirm
their own result. This keeps everyone's ratings fair.

Students get a notification when a result is recorded.

## 4. What your students see

Each student sees this on their dashboard and profile.

**Rating**
- Everyone starts at 1500. Win and your rating goes up, lose and it goes down.
  How much it moves depends on how strong your opponent is: beating a stronger
  player earns more.
- Singles and doubles have separate ratings. In doubles, the pair is rated as
  one player (average of the two partners). Both partners gain or lose the
  same number of points. A newer partner's rating stays less certain for
  longer.
- Every confirmed match counts on its own straight away. Ratings are never
  reset. They measure long-term skill.
- You are **Provisional** until you have played 10 rated matches (or the
  system is confident sooner). Provisional players show "Provisional" instead
  of a number and are listed below rated players.
- If a confirmed result is deleted, all ratings are recomputed from the match
  history, so nothing is patched by hand.
- Early ratings move a lot (a first win can be worth more than 100 points).
  They settle as students play more.

**Streak**
- Play at least one confirmed match in a week (Monday–Sunday, India time) to
  keep your streak. Your streak is the number of weeks in a row you have done
  that.
- If you have a streak of 2+ weeks and haven't played yet this week, you get a
  reminder on Saturday evening.

**Seasons**
- A season is a calendar month (India time), for each clan or batch. November
  2026 is the first season. See it on the batch's **Season** tab.
- Season points use the same rule as the leaderboard: winners gain points,
  losers lose them, and you can't go below zero. Ties are broken by more wins,
  then more matches.
- When the month ends, badges are awarded: **Season champion** (1st),
  **Top 3** (2nd and 3rd), **Most matches played**, and **Biggest rating
  gain** (rated players only, gain must be above zero). Players who tie for
  the most matches or biggest gain all get the badge.
- You get a push with your final standing and any badges. You also get at most
  one push a day per clan when someone passes you in the standings.

Students can share a match result or their rank card to WhatsApp with
**Share**.

## Notifications

Anyone can choose which notifications they get under **Notifications** (the bell
icon: in the website's menu, and at the top of the app's dashboard): match results, game sessions,
announcements, invites, streak reminders, and seasons.

## Tips for the first week

- Record results on the same day, so streaks and the season table stay
  current.
- Ask students to turn on notifications when the app asks. That is how they
  hear about results, streaks and the season.
